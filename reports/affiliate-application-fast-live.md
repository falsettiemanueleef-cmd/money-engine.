# FAST LIVE — Affiliate publisher application draft

Status: preparation only. No application has been submitted and no account has been created.

## Promotional property

- Brand: FAST LIVE
- YouTube channel: https://www.youtube.com/@fastliveglobal
- Format: original, faceless, cinematic videos and Shorts
- Languages: Italian and English
- Planned topics: practical AI tools, productivity, digital skills, useful software and realistic online opportunities
- Promotional method: educational reviews, comparisons and demonstrations; affiliate links only where the advertiser and network explicitly permit YouTube/social traffic.

## Publisher description (English)

FAST LIVE is an independent digital media project creating concise, practical videos about useful AI tools, productivity, digital skills and online services. The channel is being developed for an international audience, with content in Italian and English. Our promotional approach is educational: we aim to explain what a product or service does, who it may help, its limitations and any relevant costs before sharing a tracked link. We do not make guaranteed-income claims, use misleading endorsements, or promote products we have not assessed. Affiliate relationships will be clearly disclosed wherever links or recommendations may generate a commission.

## Publisher description (Italian)

FAST LIVE è un progetto editoriale indipendente dedicato a strumenti di intelligenza artificiale, produttività, competenze digitali e servizi online utili. Il canale si rivolge a un pubblico internazionale con contenuti in italiano e inglese. Le promozioni avranno taglio informativo: spiegheremo funzionalità, destinatari, limiti e costi dei prodotti o servizi prima di condividere eventuali link tracciati. Non promettiamo guadagni garantiti, non utilizziamo testimonianze ingannevoli e segnaleremo chiaramente ogni rapporto di affiliazione.

## Disclosure text

Italian: **Pubblicità / Link affiliato:** potremmo ricevere una commissione se effettui un acquisto tramite questo link, senza costi aggiuntivi per te.

English: **Advertising / Affiliate link:** we may earn a commission if you make a purchase through this link, at no extra cost to you.

Use the disclosure clearly near the relevant link and in the content itself. For YouTube videos containing a paid promotion or other commercial relationship, also use YouTube's paid-promotion declaration when applicable. Follow each advertiser's and network's channel-specific rules.

## Candidate networks

1. TradeTracker — publisher registration is free; channel and application require review. Network acceptance and approval for each advertiser are separate.
2. eBay Partner Network — accepts websites, social networks and apps; registration and promotional property are reviewed.
3. Amazon PartnerNet — consider after the channel has sufficient original public content and meets the programme's current eligibility rules.

## Pre-submission checks

- Confirm the public channel URL and that it is accessible.
- Publish original, relevant videos before applying if the network requires an active promotional property.
- Check each campaign's permitted traffic sources and whether YouTube/social promotion needs prior advertiser approval.
- Verify current payout threshold, payment method, validation period and any tax requirements.
- Use the publisher's real legal identity and accurate tax/payment information. The public brand may differ from the legal account holder.
- Do not submit fabricated audience, traffic, subscriber or sales figures.
- Do not submit applications, accept legal terms, or enter identity/tax/payment details without the account holder's direct participation and approval.
